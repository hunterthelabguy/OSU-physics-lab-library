Course index

Every lab below is listed under the number and title its own course summary gives it. A lab reads as a link once its handout has been converted from the original Word file and checked word for word against it. Everything else is listed so the gap is visible, not hidden.

PH 211: General Physics with Calculus

  • Lab 1: Team Building

    Discussion around inclusion; Build spaghetti towers

    Converted and checked. Also available as it was written.

  • Lab 2: Data Analysis

    Determine acceleration from data on position, focuses especially on linearizing and using graphs in Excel

    Not yet converted.

  • Lab 3: Uncertainty

    Measure a ball rolling off a ramp to understand measurement and statistical uncertainties.

    Not yet converted.

  • Lab 4: Hypothesis Testing

    Measure distance of a ball off a ramp to test two supplied hypotheses

    Not yet converted.

  • Lab 5: Designing an Experiment

    Design two experiments to measure acceleration vs. angle down a ramp; choose and perform one with reasoning behind the choice

    Not yet converted.

  • Lab 6: Describing an Experiment

    Perform one of two experiments to measure static friction, trade your procedure with a group using a different method, perform their procedure and comment on the description

    Not yet converted.

  • Lab 7: Capstone

    Two-week activity to revisit one of the previous experiments, addressing issues to improve the way the lab was done

    Not yet converted.

  • Lab Skills practical (not implemented)

    Written assessment of major skills students should have mastered throughout the curriculum

    Not yet converted.
