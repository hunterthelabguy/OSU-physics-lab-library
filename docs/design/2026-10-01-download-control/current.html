Lab 1 – Team Building

PH211 – General Physics with Calculus

Activity goal: Build community and learn how this course will work.

Working with a team is one of the most crucial skills in physics and other technical disciplines. You will likely collaborate with many people throughout your academic and non-academic careers. Employers frequently cite the ability to work well on a team as one of the most desirable traits they look for in candidates. In this lab course, all of the work is done in groups as well.

The learning objectives below give a summary of the key skills for teamwork. If you run into issues with group dynamics at any point in the course, don’t hesitate to ask your instructor for help.
