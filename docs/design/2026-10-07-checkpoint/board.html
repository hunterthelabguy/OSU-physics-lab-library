What holds for every option

  • The label word is what tells a checkpoint from a question; the colour only adds to it, so no meaning rests on colour alone.
  • Print: today's print rule draws every question with a black left rule and no fill. A checkpoint prints the same way, its label word distinguishing it, unless you want a printed difference.
  • Word: PR 2 draws questions and checkpoints as one-cell shaded tables in the same colours, for your check in Word and Google Docs.
  • Contrast is WCAG's formula on the hex values; 3:1 is the bar for a graphic such as a rule.
