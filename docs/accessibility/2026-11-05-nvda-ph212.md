# NVDA screen-reader test: PH 212 lab handouts (modern pages)

Run 2026-11-05, 11:26 to 12:14, Windows 11 10.0.26200, by a Claude
subagent driving NVDA with scripted key presses; landed under D-0166. Two
notes about the run machine's own accounts and windows are omitted here.
Fixes since: Labs 3 and 6's headings, the skip link, the footer and
navigation landmarks, and Lab 3's figure name (D-0166); Lab 6's Google
Drive link (D-0164). The math and the question labels are open.
Findings are quoted from the NVDA log, which records the speech NVDA queued,
not audio. Nothing was audible. Main-session spot checks against the built

## 1. Setup
- NVDA 2026.3 AMD64, portable copy (`--create-portable-silent
  --portable-path`). Launcher 69,352,040 bytes, signature valid (NV Access
  Limited). Flags: `-m --disable-addons --no-sr-flag --log-level=12
  --log-file --config-path`.
- Math: MathCAT 0.7.2 is built into NVDA 2026.3, no add-on. Defaults:
  ClearSpeak, Medium verbosity.
- Speech: probe session `synth = silence`; all results from eSpeak NG 1.52.0
  at `volume = 0`, verified silent offline with NVDA's espeak.dll (volume 0:
  max sample 0 over 59,440 samples; volume 100: 24905) and from NVDA's saved
  config.
- Other config: welcome, update check, usage stats off; sound volume 0;
  auto say-all off. Superscript reporting off and table headers rows and
  columns (defaults).
- Browser: Edge with a throwaway profile, `--inprivate --disable-sync
  --no-first-run --no-default-browser-check --force-renderer-accessibility`.
- Keys: `scripts\drive.py` (keybd_event, scan codes, about 0.5 s apart),
  checking before each key that the foreground window is the test Edge.
- Transcript notation: `{cap}x` capital-letter pitch, `<x>` a letter
  spoken in character mode, `..` a pause.

## 5. Problems a blind student would meet
1. "bold" spoken throughout equations (Labs 1, 2, 3, 5, 6).
2. Words inside math spelled letter by letter: total, slope, sin.
3. Malformed groupings spoken wrongly: "v is equal to times" (Lab 5),
   "1 minus times" (Lab 6), "sum from empty to empty" (Lab 3), "w star
   times" (Labs 5, 6), raw bold Unicode `𝒄𝒐𝒔` and `𝟏𝟎`.
4. Superscripts and subscripts in plain text not announced at NVDA's
   defaults: "R 2", "Δ 1".
5. Labs 3 and 6 skip from H1 to H3; appendices nest under the wrong parent.
6. "QUESTION n" labels are plain text, unreachable by H or D (Lab 1 17,
   Lab 2 9, Lab 3 24, Lab 4 11, Lab 5 8, Lab 6 10).
7. No skip link; two content-info landmarks; one unnamed navigation.
8. Vague link text: a bare URL and "here" (Lab 1); "options" (Lab 6);
   download buttons named only Word, HTML, PDF, Markdown.
9. Lab 3's figure: arrows not conveyed; an extra unnamed graphic.
10. Bullet glyphs (● ➢) read as separate lines; inline math splits a
    sentence across lines.
