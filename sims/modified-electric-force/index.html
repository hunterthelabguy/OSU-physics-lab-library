How to use

Taking measurements

  • Enter the law code your instructor assigns (0 to 10). Every row in the table records it.
  • Set q1 and q2 in coulombs: type a value (plain or scientific notation, for example 0.25 or 2.5e-1), or use the slider (0.1 C to 10 C) and the sign button.
  • Set r in meters: type it, drag charge 2 on the bench, or tap the ruler.
  • Press Take measurement (or Enter in any box). Each press adds a row to the table.

The bench

  • Keyboard: select charge 2 with Tab, then use the arrow keys. Page Up and Page Down take bigger steps; Home and End go to 0.1 m and 10 m.
  • The ruler is logarithmic: 0.1 m to 1 m is as long as 1 m to 10 m. The gap between q1 and 0.1 m is not to scale.

The table

  • The table is not saved. Copy it or download it before you close this page.
  • Copy table, then paste into cell A1 in Excel. Download CSV saves electric-force-data.csv, which Excel opens directly.
