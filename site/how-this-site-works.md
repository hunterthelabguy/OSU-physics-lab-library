# How this site works

This site holds the lab handouts for OSU's introductory physics courses,
to read on screen or download. Every reader gets the same pages: no
accounts, and nothing loaded from other sites.

## One file per handout

Each handout is kept as one plain-text file. The web page and the four
downloads (Word, PDF, HTML and the plain text itself) are all made from
that one file, so they always say the same thing.

## How a page is made

Two free, open-source tools, Pandoc and Quarto, turn those files into the
pages you see. The pages are made ahead of time and sent to your browser
as finished files: there is no database and nothing to log in to. Lab
pages are laid out for easy reading on screen, and graded questions sit
in their own labeled boxes.

## Where the site lives

The site is hosted by Cloudflare Pages. The site itself sets no cookies,
runs no analytics and loads nothing from other sites, so it learns nothing
about who reads it. Cloudflare keeps its own records under
[its privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## Search engines

For now, every page asks search engines and AI crawlers not to index or
read it.

## Who did the work

Pandoc and Quarto do most of the work. Hunter Reaves built the rest of
the site with Claude, an AI model from Anthropic, including the conversion
of the original Word documents. Every decision is his, and he answers for
the work.
